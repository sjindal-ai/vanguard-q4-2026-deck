Q4 2026 Plan

The call for Q4

Raise D0 activation and findability (mega nav + Airo), keep Homefeed client thin, and hold the installs target without staffing Acquisition yet.

Where capacity goes
Auth + Activation
Passkeys continues. BCC unlocks personalization for Logo / Brand Kit / Domains (iOS + BE first). Domain offers: AND committed, iOS stretch.
Nav / IA + Braze
My Business mega nav and Airo entry points fix findability. Ratings / feedback via Braze protects Store quality with minimal client.
Explicit trade-offs
Homefeed stays thin on client
One client bet (feedback / dismiss). Recommendation growth is BE-led personalization + new RAD candidates. Stretch delight stays off the committed line.
Acquisition not staffed yet
HRA / banner placements are sized and partner-aligned, but no Vanguard BW on them yet. Installs target still 615K + 50K; partner / DOP delivery when staffed. Settings stretch watches Manage Hub.
